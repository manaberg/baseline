BASELINE

See what your nights say

Baseline is an iPhone app. It learns your normal resting heart rate and sleeping wrist temperature from Apple Watch and shows which nights run off it. You can log drinks and mark nights you were sick. If you choose to, it can take your cycle into account.

Private by design. Baseline only reads from Apple Health and never writes to it. Everything stays on your iPhone. There's no account, no servers, no analytics and no ads.
