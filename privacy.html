BASELINE

Privacy policy

Effective 13 November 2026

In short: Baseline doesn't collect any data. Nothing you log and nothing it reads from Apple Health ever leaves your iPhone. There's no account, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

01Who we are

Baseline is an iPhone app made by Manuel Aberg Cobo, an independent developer. In this policy, "we" means Manuel Aberg Cobo. If you have a question about privacy, email manuel.acobo@gmail.com.

02Apple Health data

With your permission, Baseline reads these types of data from Apple Health:

  • Resting heart rate
  • Sleeping wrist temperature
  • Menstrual flow and ovulation test results, but only if you turn on "Use cycle data" in Baseline's Settings. It's off by default, and Baseline doesn't ask for this access until you turn it on.

Baseline uses this data only on your iPhone, to work out your personal baseline, show your charts and statistics, and line nights up with your cycle phase. It never writes anything to Apple Health. It doesn't keep its own copy of your Health data: it reads it again each time you open the app.

Your Health data is never sent to us or to anyone else. It isn't used for advertising, isn't sold and isn't shared.

03What you enter in Baseline

Baseline stores the following on your iPhone:

  • Drinks you add and the drinks you log
  • Nights you mark as sick
  • Your settings, for example whether cycle data is on

We can't see any of it. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, Apple includes this data in that backup like any other app data. That backup is controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

04What we don't do

  • Baseline makes no network requests. It doesn't send data anywhere.
  • No account or sign-in
  • No analytics, crash reporting or advertising tools, and no third-party tracking
  • We don't sell or share data, because we don't have any

05Your choices

  • Stop Baseline reading Health data: in the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps › Baseline, and turn off the types you don't want it to read.
  • Stop reading cycle data: turn off "Use cycle data" in Baseline's Settings.
  • Delete what you've logged: delete entries in the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. Deleting Baseline doesn't touch your data in Apple Health.

06Children

Baseline isn't directed at children, and its age rating on the App Store reflects that it includes references to alcohol.

07Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects how your data is handled, we'll also say so in the app's release notes.
